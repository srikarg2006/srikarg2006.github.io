Parallelizing Mixture-of-Experts Deployments using Data, Expert, and Tensor Parallelism

Evelyn Wu (evelynwu) and Srikar Govindaraju (srikarg) — 15-418/618 Final Project, Fall 2026

Summary

We will implement the forward pass of a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) layer from scratch in C++ and CUDA, distributed with MPI in multi-GPU systems on the GHC and PSC machines. Each token goes through a top-1 router, with capacity-based token dropping, and is then processed by its chosen expert FFN.

We will compare three parallelization strategies on the same model:

  • Data parallelism (DP): every GPU holds the full layer and processes its own share of tokens. This is the baseline.
  • Expert parallelism (EP): experts are split across GPUs, and tokens are sent to their expert's GPU with an MPI all-to-all.
  • Tensor parallelism (TP): every expert's weights are sliced across all GPUs, so the load stays balanced no matter how tokens are routed.

We want to explore how different routing skews change the tradeoffs. EP has less communication but is prone to load imbalances when a few experts are chosen repeatedly. TP, on the other hand, ensures a more even distribution of compuation across GPUs, but requires greater communication. We also hope to implement an architecture where we overlap communication costs with computation. Adding a backward pass, top-2 (or k) routing, and hybrid EP + IP are stretch goals that we will tackle if time permits.
